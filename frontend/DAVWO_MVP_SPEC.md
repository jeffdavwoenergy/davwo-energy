# Dashboard & Analytics Specification

**Purpose:** The Dashboard is the primary operating interface for DAVWO users. It provides monitoring, analytics, asset visibility, ANI insights, and access to marketplace services.

## Main Navigation Menu

1. Dashboard Home
2. Assets
3. Monitoring
4. Analytics
5. ANI Intelligence
6. Marketplace
7. Reports
8. Alerts Centre
9. Settings

## 1. Dashboard Home

**Purpose:** High-level overview of all connected assets and platform activity.

**Features:** Total Assets Connected · Active Assets · Energy Consumption Summary · Carbon Impact Summary · ANI Recommendations Summary · Active Alerts · System Health Status

**Expected outcome:** Users immediately understand what is happening, which assets require attention, and current performance status.

## 2. Assets

**Purpose:** Manage all energy assets connected to DAVWO.

**Asset types:** EV Chargers · Batteries (future-ready) · Solar Systems (future-ready) · Smart Energy Devices

**Features:** Asset Registration · Asset Inventory · Asset Status · Asset Location · Asset Health Score · Asset Performance Overview

**Expected outcome:** Users can view all assets, understand asset condition, and track operational performance.

## 3. Monitoring

**Purpose:** Real-time operational visibility.

**Features:** Live Asset Status · Online/Offline Monitoring · Charging Session Monitoring · System Activity Feed · Performance Indicators · Fault Detection

**Metrics:** Operational Status · Availability · Session Activity · Asset Utilisation

**Expected outcome:** Users know what is operating, what is failing, and what requires intervention.

## 4. Analytics

**Purpose:** Transform operational data into business intelligence.

- **Energy Analytics:** Energy Consumption Trends · Energy Usage History · Demand Analysis
- **Performance Analytics:** Asset Utilisation · Operational Efficiency · Performance Comparison
- **Cost Analytics:** Estimated Cost Savings · Operational Cost Insights
- **Carbon Analytics:** Carbon Intensity Tracking · Emissions Reduction Metrics

**Expected outcome:** Users understand performance trends, financial impact, and environmental impact.

## 5. ANI Intelligence

**Purpose:** The core intelligence layer of DAVWO.

- **ANI Recommendations** — e.g. "Delay charging by 2 hours to reduce carbon intensity", "Shift charging schedule to reduce costs", "Asset maintenance recommended".
- **ANI Forecasting:** Demand Forecasting · Carbon Forecasting · Utilisation Forecasting
- **ANI Insights:** Efficiency Opportunities · Cost Reduction Opportunities · Carbon Reduction Opportunities

**Expected outcome:** Users receive actionable intelligence rather than raw data.

## 6. Marketplace

**Purpose:** Connect users with energy products and services.

**MVP version:** Marketplace **Preview only**.

**Categories:** EV Chargers · Battery Solutions · Solar Solutions · Energy Services · Consulting Services

**Features:** Product Listings · Service Listings · Vendor Profiles · Contact Vendor

**Explicitly NOT in MVP:** Payments · Transactions · Order Processing

**Expected outcome:** Users discover solutions and service providers through DAVWO.

## 7. Reports

**Purpose:** Generate business and operational reports.

**Report types:** Daily · Weekly · Monthly · Custom
**Export formats:** PDF · CSV
**Report categories:** Energy Reports · Asset Reports · Carbon Reports · ANI Reports

**Expected outcome:** Users can share and analyse operational performance.

## 8. Alert Centre

**Purpose:** Central location for all notifications and operational alerts.

**Alert types:** Asset Offline · Performance Degradation · Fault Detection · Maintenance Alerts · ANI Recommendations · System Notifications

**Features:** Alert History · Alert Priority Levels · Alert Resolution Tracking

**Expected outcome:** Users respond quickly to operational issues.

## 9. Settings

**Purpose:** Manage platform configuration and user preferences.

- **Account Settings:** Profile Management · Password Management
- **Organisation Settings:** Company Information · User Management
- **Platform Settings:** Notification Preferences · Dashboard Preferences

**Expected outcome:** Users maintain control of platform configuration.

# MVP Success Criteria

The MVP is successful if users can:

1. Log into the platform.
2. View connected assets.
3. Monitor system activity.
4. Access analytics and reports.
5. Receive ANI recommendations.
6. Explore marketplace offerings.
7. Export reports.
8. Manage alerts and settings.

**Strategic outcome:** Demonstrate DAVWO's ability to combine monitoring, analytics, AI-driven optimisation, and marketplace access within a single Climate AI Infrastructure platform.

# Demonstration Environment

**Purpose:** The first public showcase of the DAVWO platform — demonstrating how organisations can monitor energy assets, analyse operational performance, receive ANI-powered optimisation insights, and access energy solutions through a single intelligent platform.

Must be professional, intuitive, visually impressive, and presentable to investors, pilot customers, partners, universities, and industry stakeholders.

## Demo Structure

1. **Login Screen** — email/password login, demo account access, DAVWO branding. Enterprise-grade appearance.
2. **Dashboard Home** — total connected assets, active assets, energy consumption, carbon impact, active alerts, ANI recommendations. KPI cards, summary charts, system health indicators.
3. **Asset Management** — inventory, status, health, location, performance. EV Chargers + Batteries (Preview) + Solar (Preview).
4. **Monitoring Centre** — live asset status, online/offline indicators, charging activity, operational events, performance metrics.
5. **Analytics Centre** — energy consumption trends, utilisation, operational performance; carbon intensity + emissions-reduction opportunities; estimated savings + efficiency improvements.
6. **ANI Intelligence Centre** — recommendations (delay charging, optimise schedules, improve utilisation, reduce costs), forecasts (demand/carbon/utilisation), insights (efficiency/cost/carbon opportunities).
7. **Marketplace Preview** — vendor, product, and service listings across EV charging, battery, solar, and energy services. No payments or transactions.
8. **Reports Centre** — daily/weekly/monthly reports, PDF + CSV export.
9. **Alert Centre** — asset alerts, system alerts, ANI recommendations, maintenance notifications.

## Demo Success Criteria

Users can: log in · view energy assets · monitor operational activity · analyse performance data · receive ANI recommendations · generate reports · explore marketplace opportunities.

**Most importantly:** users leave with a clear understanding that DAVWO is building an intelligent climate and energy infrastructure platform powered by ANI.
