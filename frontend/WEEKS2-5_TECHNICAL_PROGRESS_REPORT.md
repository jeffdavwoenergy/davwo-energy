### Week 6 (16 September, in progress) — ANI™ Intelligence Foundation: model evaluation

The directive scopes this week to Jeff (Lead) and Amirreza (Potential Energy AI Lead), with "Data/Backend Developer" as support — Amirreza's engagement is still subject to the technical assessment referenced in Section 4, so this window's contribution is scoped deliberately to the support role, not to architecture decisions that are genuinely Jeff's/Amirreza's to make.

Of the directive's six named Week 6 focus areas (technical framework, data-to-intelligence pipeline, model integration, analytical logic, recommendation framework, **model evaluation**, intelligence outputs), the first five substantially already existed — the one genuine gap was **model evaluation**: nothing anywhere in the codebase measured whether ANI's forecast is actually accurate. Both `Insight.confidence` and `Forecast`'s band width were (and for insights, still are) functions of sample size, never of measured predictive accuracy.

- **Shipped:** `src/lib/ani/evaluation.ts` — genuine walk-forward backtesting of the real production `forecast()` function (not a reimplementation of it): at each historical cutoff date it forecasts using only data available up to that point, then scores the prediction against what actually happened, reporting MAE/MAPE/confidence-band coverage per lead-time horizon. Wired through a new provider function, a new `GET /api/ani/forecast-accuracy` endpoint, a "Forecast Accuracy" panel on `/forecasting`, and a new ANI assistant capability so a user asking "how accurate is the forecast?" gets an evidence-based answer instead of a repeat of the same sample-size-derived confidence figure. Live-verified: 1-day-ahead forecasts backtest to ~3.6% mean error, landing inside the forecast's own band ~97–100% of the time — notably *higher* than the page's stated "80% confidence band" target, meaning the band is currently conservative rather than miscalibrated the other way. 9 new tests, full coverage gate still passing (99.01%/88.85%/100%/99.78%).
- **Explicitly not attempted:** calibrating `Insight.confidence` (capacity_risk / rising_demand / underutilised / recurring_fault). Those insight types have no automatic, unambiguous "did it happen" ground-truth signal in this synthetic dataset — scoring them would mean inventing a success criterion, which this codebase's own data sources deliberately avoid doing (see the honest-`null` pattern in `src/lib/data/regions/us.ts`). Designing what "correct" means for a rule-based insight is real Jeff/Amirreza architecture work, not something to fabricate as a backend task.
- **Still not started:** the actual Jeff/Amirreza-led architecture review of the existing ANI framework the directive's Week 6 wants (a deliberate design pass, not just an accuracy measurement bolted onto what already exists) — and Amirreza's own technical assessment/engagement, which several of Week 6/7's other focus areas depend on.
